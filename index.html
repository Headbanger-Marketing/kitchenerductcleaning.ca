How this site handles inquiries

Does one load need another drying cycle?

A dryer vent’s cleaning scope depends on its route, length, bends, material, and access to the exterior termination. For a Kitchener home with an interior laundry area, a long run, or several elbows, ask whether the proposed work follows the complete line from the dryer connection to the outside hood. Photograph the dryer connection and exterior cap if accessible, and note how long drying takes. This information helps compare cleaning methods and determine whether restricted airflow may need further investigation.

What details help with a Kitchener quote?

Compare proposals for the cleaning method and the sections included. A negative-pressure setup with contact brushing is one approach: registers are removed, branches are brushed, and suction draws loosened debris toward collection equipment. Ask how the return side, trunk lines, and debris containment are handled so you can understand what the quoted scope covers. Photos can help you review visible duct interiors, but they should correspond to your system and the work included. Ask which locations will be photographed and whether the images will be reviewed with you.
